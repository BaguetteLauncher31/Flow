# Flow
Flow - A game where you have to beat the current!


Basically, what you're gonna wanna do is avoid the light blue obstacles, and then hit the dark blue ones. Be careful though, they're not visible for 1/5th of the screen!
Getting a dark blue square results in 1000 points being added to your score, a major bonues.
That's the whole point of the game, it'll be improved as time goes on.

Good Luck Sailor!
